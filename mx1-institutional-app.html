Step 3 of 5 — Where your assets live

Connect your custody

Two ways to hold your capital — both keep MX1 trade-only, never able to withdraw. Pick the model your mandate prefers.

On-chain · self-custody — you hold the keys

Your assets sit in the MX1 vault contract your own wallet controls. Trades settle on-chain via DEXs. No third party ever holds them.
